Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, amazing sunsets, and delicious local food.

PLACES TO VISIT

Jaipur

Jaipur is known for its royal palaces, historic forts, and colorful traditional markets.

PLACES TO VISIT
  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
